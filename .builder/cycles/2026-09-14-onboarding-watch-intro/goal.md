# Goal

- User problem: after PR #45 collapsed onboarding to a single publication-picker page, new users meet the Apple Watch value proposition for the first time inside the Reader — a small card competing with the book they just opened. The core differentiator gets no dedicated moment.
- Target user: new iPhone users in the target wedge (readers who want hands-free page turning with Apple Watch) during first-run onboarding.
- Desired behavior: after choosing a publication, iPhone users see one focused Watch value page before the Reader opens, so they arrive at the Reader's activation card already understanding what the Watch does and why it matters.
- Success: iPhone onboarding shows the Watch intro page between publication selection and the Reader; the Reader activation card still appears afterward; iPad onboarding is unchanged; regression tests cover the new step.
- Out of scope: reading-goal/reminder pages, Pro upsell pages, notification permission prompts, iPad onboarding changes, Watch app install flow changes.
