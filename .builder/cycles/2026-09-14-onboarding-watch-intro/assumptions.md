# Assumptions

- A dedicated value page raises Watch activation (first Watch page turn) more than it costs in onboarding drop-off.
- Users who reach the Reader already understanding the Watch feature are less likely to dismiss the activation card reflexively.
- One page is below the annoyance threshold; the pre-#45 flow had up to three pages, so this stays well under the historical maximum.
- Existing evidence: the pre-#45 three-page flow existed for the entire 1.0.13–1.0.20 period without recorded drop-off complaints; the Reader card alone (post-#45) gives users no context for why they should care.
- Falsifier: activation rate (Watch Setup Completion among new installs) does not improve, or onboarding abandonment between publication selection and Reader rises after release.
