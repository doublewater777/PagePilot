# Assumptions

- Leading with a dedicated Apple Watch value intro page establishes context and desire before asking users to import a book.
- Making book selection the final onboarding step gives direct momentum into reading immediately after choosing a book, rather than interrupting the user with an intro page after they already picked what to read.
- Users who reach the Reader already understand why the Watch card matters.
- One page is below the annoyance threshold; the pre-#45 flow had up to three pages, so this stays well under the historical maximum.
- Existing evidence: the pre-#45 three-page flow existed for the entire 1.0.13–1.0.20 period without recorded drop-off complaints; leading with the core differentiator frames why PagePilot exists.
- Falsifier: activation rate (Watch Setup Completion among new installs) does not improve, or onboarding abandonment rises after release.
