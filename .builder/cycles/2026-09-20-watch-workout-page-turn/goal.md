# Goal

- User problem: during an Apple Watch Workout, wrist-down / wrist-up can return to Workout and make PagePilot's foreground-only crown, Double Tap, and buttons unavailable.
- Target user: readers using PagePilot on iPhone or iPad while recording a workout on Apple Watch.
- Desired behavior: preserve a discoverable workaround and expose previous/next controls from the Reading Live Activity in the Watch Smart Stack.
- Success: Workout guidance is available in Watch Settings, Smart Stack controls route through the existing Reader pipeline, and one interaction produces at most one turn per active route.
- Out of scope: fake workout sessions, private APIs, forcing PagePilot to stay frontmost, changing the minimum OS, or background auto-page-turning.
